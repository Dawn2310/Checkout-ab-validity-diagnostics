📋 Three Research Questions

RQ1 — Aggregate Comparison: What does the pooled click-level conversion comparison show, and how does the conclusion change when the analysis unit shifts from clicks to calendar days?

RQ2 — Reliability: What do overdispersion modelling, sample-ratio-mismatch diagnostics, and A/A simulation reveal about the reliability of naive inference on this aggregate dataset?

RQ3 — Interpretation: What descriptive funnel and weekday patterns emerge, and why should they be treated as hypothesis-generating rather than causal evidence?
