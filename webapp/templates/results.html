Why Do the Two Views Contradict?

This is the unit-of-analysis paradox. The massive click denominator (~338K) shrinks the standard error of the Z-test to near zero, so any difference becomes "significant". But the true replicated units visible in the public data are 30 days per group — and between days, CR fluctuates wildly due to ad delivery algorithms, weekday seasonality, spend variation, and unobserved traffic composition.

The quasi-binomial model directly estimates this variance inflation (scale = Pearson χ² / residual df = 162.95) and expands standard errors accordingly. Result: p = 0.342 — the data cannot conclude that Test is genuinely worse.
